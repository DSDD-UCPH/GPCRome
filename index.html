This GPCR-tree mapper (GPCRome mapper) is developed by Albert J. Kooistra and Chris de Graaf.
The tree is based on the modified GPCR tree presented by Stevens and Katritch et al. and the original tree by Fredriksson et al.. It was updated using refined sequence alignments (using available structures of GPCRs) focusing mainly on the sequence of the 7TM bundle. GPCR names have been updated, references sequences were updated from the latest UniProt, new GPCRs were added, and obsoleted sequences were removed.
